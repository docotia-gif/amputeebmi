Privacy Policy

Amputee BMI mobile app · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Amputee BMI mobile app ("the App") handles information when you use it on your iPhone or iPad. It applies to the native app distributed through Apple's App Store. For the web calculator and other pages on amputeebmi.com, see our website Privacy Policy.

Information the App does not collect

The App is a calculator. The height, weight, and amputation-level values you enter are used only to perform the calculation on your device. They are never transmitted to us, stored on our servers, written to any server-side database, or shared with any third party.

The App has no account system, no login, and no analytics that profile your use of it. It contains no health records, no medical claims, and no user-generated content. You can delete the App at any time, which removes all local data.

Advertising

The App is supported by advertising and is provided at no cost. Advertising is served by Google AdMob, and Google and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device information to serve and measure ads.

Where required by law — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — Google's certified consent platform (UMP) is presented before personalized advertising is served. If you decline or withdraw consent, you will continue to receive non-personalized ads and the App will function normally. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Privacy or Settings app.

We do not control the content of advertisements shown by third parties. Personalized advertising is not enabled for users under the applicable age of consent.

Health and medical disclaimer

The App is a reference tool for general wellness information. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and it does not substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a physician, dietitian, or other qualified health professional for guidance specific to your situation.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact
