Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how amputeebmi.com ("we," "us," or "the site") handles information when you use our BMI calculator and related pages.

Information we do not collect

The BMI calculator runs entirely in your browser. The height, weight, and amputation-level values you enter are used only to perform the calculation on your device and are never transmitted to us, stored on our servers, or shared with any third party. We do not require an account, login, or any personal health information to use the calculator.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and manage your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We have applied content blocking controls in our AdSense settings to exclude gambling, dating, alcohol, and other categories inconsistent with a clinical reference tool, and we periodically review live ad placements for quality.

Analytics

We may use standard, privacy-respecting web analytics to understand aggregate site traffic (e.g., page views, general geographic region, device type). This data is anonymized/aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors or linked to any health information entered into the calculator.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party resources (e.g., research citations). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
